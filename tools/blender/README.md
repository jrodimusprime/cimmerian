# The cast pipeline: CC0 bodies in headless Blender

`game/models/cast/*.glb` is built here. Every man in the cast starts as a real human body from **MakeHuman (MPFB)**. Armour, helms, masks, robes, beasts and weapons are then built over it by script. Nothing is modelled by hand, and every input is public domain (CC0).

## What it uses, and the licences

| Input | From | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Base mesh, shape targets (body, face, expressions), game-engine skeleton and its weights | [MPFB](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb.html), the MakeHuman extension for Blender | **CC0**. The base mesh, targets and skeletons are graphical assets ([MPFB FAQ](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/faq/can_i_sell_models.html)). MPFB's *code* is GPL, but it is a tool and isn't shipped |
| Skins (young, middle-aged and old; caucasian, african, asian), eyebrows, hair (long, short, ponytail) | [MakeHuman system assets](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html) | **CC0** (`makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip`) |
| More skins (a bearded Slavic man for the Shemites, an old Eurasian man for Tsotha) | [Skins 02](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/skins02.html) | **CC0** (`skins02_cc0.zip`; every entry is marked CC0 in `skins02.json`) |
| The war-horse and Tsotha's steed: [Rigged Horse](https://opengameart.org/content/rigged-horse) (`riggedHorse.blend`, its painted hide, mane, tail, eyes and rig) | OpenGameArt | **CC0** |

MakeHuman's exports carry no licence claim from the MakeHuman project: the resulting models are ours to ship.

## Running it

```sh
# once: a private Blender profile with MPFB installed (your own Blender settings are untouched)
export MPFB_ENV=/some/dir/benv
BLENDER_USER_RESOURCES=$MPFB_ENV /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --online-mode \
  --command extension install --sync --enable mpfb
# once: the CC0 asset packs, unzipped side by side (use the files. mirror; files2. is very slow)
export MH_ASSETS=/some/dir/mhassets
curl -LO https://files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip   # unzip into $MH_ASSETS/sys
curl -LO https://files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/skins02/skins02_cc0.zip                                   # unzip into $MH_ASSETS/skins

export HORSE_BLEND=/some/dir/riggedHorse.blend    # https://opengameart.org/content/rigged-horse (CC0)
export GOBLIN_BLEND=/some/dir/goblin-FINAL.blend  # https://opengameart.org/content/goblin-1 (CC0)
export COBRA_BLEND=/some/dir/cobra.blend          # https://opengameart.org/content/cobra-0 (CC0)
export PHOTO_TEX=/some/dir/tex     # ambientCG Leather008_1K-JPG and Leather037_1K-JPG, each unzipped into its own folder (CC0)
./run.sh                       # every man in casting.CAST
./run.sh kknight shukeli       # just these
./run.sh horse steed thing thing_b thing_c weeper_body weeper_tentacle weeper_head yothga_stem yothga_tendril yothga_bloom eagle_body eagle_wing
./run.sh w_sword w_greatsword w_dagger w_axe w_spear w_pike w_bow
```

Then open `game/index.html?cast` to see them, front and profile.

## How a man is made (`casting.py`, `body.py`)

1. **Body.** MPFB's macros (gender, age, muscle, weight, height, proportions, ethnic mix) set the build, plus extra targets per character. Conan and the giants get a V-shape and heavy pectorals, lats and arms. Shukeli gets a pot belly and a double chin. The pit-things get a long skull, an open jaw and long arms.
2. **Hair and brows.** These are CC0 MakeHuman assets fitted to that body. Their own textures are kept and tinted, and they draw as alpha-tested cards.
3. **Rest pose.** He stands straight with arms hanging and fists closed, and that becomes the rest pose. The finger bones are folded into the hands, which leaves 23 bones. The game's pose library (`P.lean`, `P.uxR`, …) drives those joints directly (`game/js/skinned.js`).
4. **Outfit (`gear.py`).**
   - Garments are cut from the body itself: the faces under the right bones, pushed out along the normals and relaxed so cloth hangs rather than clings. They keep the body's skin weights.
   - Helms are a bowl cast from the skull. **Face masks are cast from the face itself, with the eyes cut out**, so each masked knight wears a steel death-mask of a real face.
   - Shields, pauldrons and helms ride one bone each.
5. **Bake (`outfit.py`).** Each surface's recipe (tint × tiled pattern × object-space grime × worn edges) and the whole figure's ambient occlusion are baked by Cycles into one texture per object.
6. **Decimate and export** as `.glb`.

The horses and the pit-things are downloaded CC0 models (above), refitted in `beasts.py` to the bones the game drives: the horse's legs, neck and tail for the gallop, the goblin's skeleton folded down to the cast's 23 bones so the pit-things' crawl and strike poses drive it. The other beasts use metaballs (the weeping thing) or tubes and leaves (Yothga, the eagle). They are static parts that the game animates piece by piece.
